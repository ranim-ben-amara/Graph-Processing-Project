Who are we?

We are an ISS196 group at Medtech University and we wanted to create a new platform to connect people through their hobbies.The MedTech Software Engineering Curriculum offers solid training to engineering students on the theoretical and technological elements of stable high-performance software systems. The curriculum also teaches students on the management aspects of software engineering, including project management and team management. The Software Engineering Curriculum is built on a strong basis in the general fields of hardware and software applications and networks, and provides specialized training in software processes and management, software development and design trends, and software quality assurance.
